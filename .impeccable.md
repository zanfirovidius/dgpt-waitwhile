## Design Context

### Users
Admins and a small core group of people who organize, maintain, and oversee projects. They use the product in an operational context, where they need to configure resources quickly, verify setup, and keep day-by-day execution under control without hunting for information.

### Brand Personality
Clean, easy to use, and operationally clear. The interface should feel calm and dependable rather than flashy, with strong scanability and obvious next actions.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light admin interface with restrained visual styling, clear grouping, and stronger hierarchy through spacing and structure rather than decorative effects. No specific visual references were provided. Avoid cluttered dashboard density, generic card spam, or marketing-style hero treatments.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize scanability over ornament: important information should be identifiable within seconds.
2. Group by workflow: setup, daily scheduling, and derived outputs should read as distinct phases.
3. Use space deliberately: tight grouping for related controls, generous separation between sections.
4. Keep controls obvious: inputs, summaries, and actions should have clear hierarchy and low ambiguity.
5. Preserve a calm admin tone: light theme, restrained accents, and minimal visual noise.
